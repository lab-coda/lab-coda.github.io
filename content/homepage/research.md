Our research broadly fits into these three areas:

#### Evaluation of neurocognitive brain models

How do we evaluate self-supervised models of brain activity, cognition, and day-to-day experience that are both powerful and interpretable, using naturalistic neuroimaging?


#### Multimodal mental health indicators

How do we deploy these models as symptom-first predictors that go beyond diagnostic labels to shed light on the neurocognitive mechanisms underlying mental health conditions?

#### Reproducible clinical data science

Clinical datasets present unique constraints, including limited sample sizes and privacy requirements. 
How do we evaluate data-intensive models appropriately and build tools that enable open sharing without compromising patient privacy?
